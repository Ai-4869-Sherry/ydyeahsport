ABOUT YD

Born From Passion, Driven By Innovation

Who are we?

Over a decade ago, our founder, Ken Liu, took a paddleboard out onto the open water and realized a major gap in the market: existing gear simply wasn't built for real outdoor adventures. Driven by his passion for water sports and a desire for better equipment, Ken established YD Yeah Sport with a clear mission—to engineer world-class SUP and kayak paddles that seamlessly combine advanced tech with practical design, sharing the spirit of adventure with enthusiasts worldwide.

Operating as a close-knit family business, true teamwork is at the heart of everything we do. Every member of our team is dedicated to a shared goal: crafting strong, highly durable products that help our clients build outstanding brand reputations globally. When you partner with us, you gain more than a contract manufacturer—you team up with experts who live the sport and stand behind every paddle we make.

Every Stroke Counted

2012

The Spark of YD

Driven by a lifelong passion for the water, Ken Liu founded YD with a vision born on the open waves. The name represents “Yue Dong” (悦动)—translated as Joy in Motion—a philosophy that lies at the heart of everything we build. Ken believed that bad equipment should never compromise a great session. He set out to craft high-performance paddles that give water sports enthusiasts the freedom to push their limits, connect with nature, and confidently embark on every new adventure.

2016

Taking Flight

The emergence of hydrofoils changed the game, giving paddlers the magic of lifting clear off the water. Catching this wave early, YD formed a dedicated team to craft our own original hydrofoil gear. Through countlessly tweaking designs, testing on the water, and dialing in every detail, we made flying above the waves smoother, safer, and more fun. This breakthrough opened up an exciting new dimension for our global partners and water lovers everywhere.

2024

Agile Growth

Listening closely to our partners' needs, YD expanded into new accessories by developing our high-performance spray decks. By quickly reconfiguring our machinery and production lines, our team delivered perfected prototypes in record time, earning high praise from our clients. This agility not only broadened our product matrix, but also reinforced YD’s commitment to providing a seamless, one-stop sourcing experience for global brands.

2026

A Visual Leap

At YD, we have always let our manufacturing quality speak for itself—but we knew our visual presentation needed to match our engineering excellence. We wanted our partners to truly feel the premium value of our gear before they even hit the water. This year, we poured our energy into a complete overhaul of our website and product catalogs. Drawing inspiration from world-class international brands, we crafted a sleek, modern visual identity of our own. It's more than just a new look; it's a reflection of the craftsmanship and forward-thinking spirit behind everything we make.
